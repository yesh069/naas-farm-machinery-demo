TAMS 3 · 2023–2027

TAMS 3 grants and eligible machinery.

The Targeted Agricultural Modernisation Scheme provides grant aid of 40–60% toward eligible farm machinery, equipment and infrastructure.

40–60%grant aid toward eligible costs
2023–27the current scheme period
Tranchesapplications open periodically with closing dates

Who qualifies?

TAMS 3 is open to all registered herd owners in Ireland. Higher grant rates of up to 60% are available for young farmers, women in agriculture, and certain scheme categories including organic farming.

What machinery is eligible?

A wide range of agricultural machinery and equipment qualifies under TAMS 3, including:

  • Slurry equipment
  • Fencing and water systems
  • Rainwater harvesting
  • Animal welfare equipment
  • Tillage equipment
  • Energy efficiency measures
  • Safety equipment

Many of the machinery brands we stock are TAMS 3 eligible. Ask in store, or send us the model you are looking at.

How do tranches work?

Applications open in tranches throughout the year with specific closing dates. Contact us to find out the current tranche status and closing date.

How can Naas Farm Machinery help?

We have been helping Kildare and Leinster farmers with TAMS grant-eligible machinery since the scheme began. Our team can:

  • Advise on which machinery qualifies under TAMS 3
  • Help you identify the right equipment for your farm
  • Support you through the purchase process once approval is granted
