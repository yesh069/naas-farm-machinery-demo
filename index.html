Our story

Four decades, one family, the same yard.

Naas Farm Machinery was established by George Delahunt in 1986. Today it is run by his two sons, Donal and Gary, with mechanics who each bring over 30 years of experience, an extensive stores and parts department and a large yard of new and second-hand machines.

We supply machines to farmers, the equine community and everyone in between.

Why Naas Farm Machinery

The machine is the start of the relationship.

  • Family-run, locally known

    You deal with the people whose name is over the gate. Customers describe the staff as friendly, knowledgeable and helpful.

  • Our own workshop

    A 7-bay workshop with 5 mechanics, for agricultural and construction machinery, across all major brands.

  • Call-out across Leinster

    Four service vans cover Kildare, Dublin, Wicklow, Carlow and Laois, with 24-hour call-out available on request.

  • Stores and parts

    An extensive parts department, so the part you need is usually on the shelf.

  • Help with TAMS 3

    We advise on which machinery qualifies and support the purchase once approval is granted.

TAMS 3 · 2023–2027

Grant aid on eligible machinery.

  • Open to registered herd owners in Ireland
  • Higher rates for young farmers, women in agriculture and certain categories
  • Applications open in tranches with set closing dates
